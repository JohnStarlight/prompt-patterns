# Summarization Pattern

## Text 1 (Technical):
Quantum computing utilizes qubits, which, unlike classical bits that are either 0 or 1, can exist in multiple states simultaneously due to superposition. This allows for massive parallel processing. However, the hardware is extremely fragile; even minor temperature fluctuations can cause 'decoherence,' or data loss. For instance, IBM uses massive dilution refrigerators to keep chips colder than outer space. Despite these hurdles, the potential for drug discovery and cryptography is revolutionary.

## Prompt: Summarize this text.

### Answer:
Quantum computing uses qubits and superposition to process data in ways classical computers can't. However, the hardware is very fragile and requires extreme cooling to prevent data loss. While difficult to build, it could revolutionize fields like medicine and security.

## Better Prompt: Summarize this text in 3 bullet points. Focus on the main arguments and ignore examples.

### Answer:
* Computational Advantage: Quantum computing leverages superposition to perform massive parallel processing, exceeding the binary limitations of classical bits.

* Environmental Instability: The primary technical hurdle is extreme hardware fragility, where minor external fluctuations lead to decoherence and data loss.

* Strategic Potential: If stability challenges are overcome, the technology offers revolutionary advancements in specialized fields like cryptography and drug discovery.

## Text 2 (Political):
The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) shutdown has officially become the longest partial shutdown in U.S. history as of this Sunday. While the House passed a stopgap bill on Friday, it was rejected by the Senate due to disputes over ICE funding. Simultaneously, "No Kings" protests drew an estimated 8 million people across 50 states on Saturday, protesting the administration’s war in Iran and rising living costs. For example, in Minneapolis, 200,000 people filled the streets, and in West Palm Beach, police had to de-escalate a clash between protesters and a small group of Proud Boys.

## Prompt: Summarize this text.

### Answer:
The DHS shutdown is now the longest in history because the House and Senate can't agree on a funding bill. At the same time, millions of people joined "No Kings" protests across the country on Saturday to speak out against the war in Iran and high prices. There were big crowds in places like Minneapolis and some tension with counter-protesters in Florida.

## Better Prompt: Summarize this text in 3 bullet points. Focus on the main arguments and ignore examples.

### Answer:
* Record-Breaking Gridlock: The partial shutdown of the Department of Homeland Security has reached a historic duration due to a legislative impasse between the House and Senate over specific agency funding.
* Massive Civil Unrest: The "No Kings" movement mobilized millions of citizens globally to voice a collective opposition to the administration's current military and economic policies.
* Multi-Issue Dissent: The core grievances driving the protests center on the escalating conflict in Iran and the domestic impact of rising inflation and living costs.
